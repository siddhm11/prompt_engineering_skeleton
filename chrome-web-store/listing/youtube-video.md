# Promo video

The store takes a **YouTube URL**, not a file. Upload
`video/prompt-memory-demo-1080p.mp4` (46 s, 1920×1080, H.264, 30 fps, no
audio) to YouTube, then paste its link into **Store listing → Global promo
video**.

## Upload settings

- **Visibility:** Public or Unlisted. The store accepts both; a Private video won't play on the listing.
- **Made for kids:** No
- **Thumbnail:** `video/youtube-thumbnail-1920x1080.png`. Custom thumbnails need a phone-verified YouTube account.
- **Music:** the video is silent. Adding a track from the YouTube Audio Library in YouTube Studio → Editor keeps it copyright-safe.
